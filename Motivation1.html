✨ Dealer Charitha ✨

Sometimes people speak carelessly, throwing words like stones without thinking about the heart they might hit.

But remember this — the opinions of people who never truly cared about you are like passing clouds; they make noise for a moment and then disappear.

Your kindness is beautiful, but never let anyone take advantage of it. Even the softest river knows when to become strong.

Do not shrink your heart because someone failed to understand it. A diamond does not lose its value just because someone couldn't recognize it.

Stay kind, stay strong — but remember, not everyone deserves the goodness you carry inside you.

And if the world ever feels heavy, remember you always have people who stand beside you like mountains that never move.
